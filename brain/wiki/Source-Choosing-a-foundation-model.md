# Source: Choosing a foundation model in watsonx.ai

## Summary
Guidance on factors to consider when selecting a foundation model in [[IBM watsonx.ai]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Selection Factors**: Benchmarks, context window length, and modality (text/image).
- **Model Categories**: Chat, Tool interaction, and [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)]].
- **Granite Models**: IBM's flagship series supporting various tasks including RAG and Tool-calling.
- **Multimodal Models**: Support image-to-text tasks (visual Q&A, chart interpretation).
