# Tokens

Tokens are the basic units of text processed by foundation models.

## Characteristics
- **Size**: Approximately 10 words correspond to 15-20 tokens.
- **Variability**: Tokenization depends on the specific model and language used.
- **Limits**: Every model has a "Context Window" limit (input + output).

## Management
- **Prompt Lab**: Shows token usage per submission.
- **API**: The `Text tokenization` method allows pre-calculating token counts.
- **Billing**: Measured in increments (e.g., 1,000 tokens).
